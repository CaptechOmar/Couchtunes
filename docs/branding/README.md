## Credit for the Linux logo

`couchtunes-linux.svg` is based on the Apple Music icon from [MoreWaita](https://github.com/somepaulo/MoreWaita)
(`scalable/apps/apple-music.svg`) by somepaulo and contributors. It keeps that icon's background and gradient, and the music
note on top was replaced with Couchtunes' own note.

MoreWaita is licensed under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/somepaulo/MoreWaita/blob/main/LICENSE),
so the logo files listed above (the SVG and the images made from it) are used and shared under the same license. The rest of
Couchtunes, including its code, stays under the [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

Couchtunes is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple. The icon only describes the look of the app.
